Download "Patiences Application"

Playing Patiences Application :
• Only use the blue Menu button inside the window, top-left.
• Don't move the window by using its bar (you can try, very amusing !). If you really need, click outside the window, then move the window, then click inside and push cmd/P to unpause. Don't forget that each time you get out of the app and back, you must unpause by cmd/P ! (TNT feature...).
• Sometimes, you will get an alert, I don't know why... Try to answer it, but generally, the app crashes... (TNT feature ?)
• If you use English language, build the App from the Code, after changing false to true for the variable "english" second line of Global variables... then english will become the default language of the app.

When you launch the app, wait till cards are loaded. Choose another Patience than Lido in the left of the menu if you want. Manx is more simple, and more often successful.
You have a full Undo (cmd/Z) and Redo (cmd/E) capability. Attention : cmd/R relaunches the app from zero (TNT feature). Short-cuts and rules (general and particular) are accessed through the Menu.
When the soft finds how to finish the patience, it runs alone by itself. But you can still use Menu during this time.
